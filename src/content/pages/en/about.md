## SC Bodenmais — a ski club with heart

SC Bodenmais is a traditional sports club from the heart of the Bavarian Forest. Alongside winter sports, the club has created a summer highlight with the **Arberblitz** — a race that attracts cyclists and runners from the region and beyond.

## The Race

The **Arberblitz** — also known as the Sommerblitz am Arber — is an uphill time trial held every autumn. The route runs from the SC Bodenmais clubhouse in Bodenmais (750 m above sea level) up to Chamer Hütte on the Großer Arber (1,300 m above sea level).

- **Start:** SC Bodenmais Clubhouse, Bodenmais (750 m)
- **Finish:** Chamer Hütte (1,300 m)
- **Distance:** approx. 8 km
- **Elevation gain:** 550 m
- **Date 2026:** 17 October 2026

## The Categories

**Mountain Bike** — The classic uphill time trial on an MTB, without e-bike assistance.

**Gravel Bike** — Separate classification for gravel bikers on the same route, also without motor assistance.

**Trail Running** — Those who prefer to conquer mountains on foot compete in the running category.

## SC Bodenmais

Our club stands for community, fairness and a passion for movement in nature. Whether competitive athlete or leisure rider — everyone is welcome at Arberblitz. We look forward to seeing you on 17 October 2026!
